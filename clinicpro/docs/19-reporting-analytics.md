# 19 — Reporting & Analytics

## Available Reports

### Revenue Reports
- Daily revenue
- Monthly revenue
- Revenue by doctor
- Revenue by service type
- Payment method breakdown
- Outstanding/pending collections

### Appointment Reports
- Daily appointment count
- Doctor-wise appointment distribution
- Status breakdown (completed, cancelled, no-show)
- Peak hours analysis

### Patient Reports
- New registrations (daily/monthly)
- Gender distribution
- Age distribution
- Geographic distribution (city/state)
- Active vs inactive patients

### Doctor Reports
- Consultation count per doctor
- Revenue generated per doctor
- Commission calculations
- Attendance/availability

### Inventory Reports
- Stock valuation
- Low stock items
- Expiry alerts
- Purchase vs sales analysis

### Financial Reports
- Profit & loss overview
- GST summary
- Tax collection report
- Refund report

## Report Features

- Date range filtering
- Export to CSV/Excel
- Print-ready layouts
- Chart visualizations (Chart.js)
- Permission-based access (reports.view, reports.export)

## Dashboard Analytics

- Revenue chart (last 7 days)
- Today's appointment stats
- Patient growth trend
- Collection status
