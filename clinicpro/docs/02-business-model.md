# 02 — Business Model

## Productization Strategy

- Build Once
- White-label
- License Activate
- Sell Unlimited Times

## License Types

| Plan | Doctors | Patients | Price (INR) |
|------|---------|----------|-------------|
| Starter | 1 | 500 | ₹10,000 |
| Professional | 5 | Unlimited | ₹25,000 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Unlimited | ₹50,000 |

## Revenue Streams

| Stream | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Software License | ₹10,000 – ₹50,000 |
| Setup/Installation Fee | ₹2,000 – ₹20,000 |
| Annual Maintenance (AMC) | 20% of license |
| Hosting (optional) | ₹500/month |
| Custom Features | Per-quote |
| SMS/WhatsApp Credits | Usage-based |

## Sales Channels

1. Direct sales (website)
2. Digital marketing (Google Ads, SEO)
3. Channel partners / resellers
4. Freelancer marketplaces (Codecanyon, etc.)

## Cost Structure

- Hosting: ₹500–2000/month (for demo/license server)
- Development: One-time
- Support: Part-time
- Marketing: Variable

## Scaling Strategy

Phase 1: Sell licenses manually
Phase 2: Automated license portal
Phase 3: SaaS model with subscription
Phase 4: Marketplace with plugins/add-ons
